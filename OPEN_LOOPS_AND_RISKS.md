# Open Loops and Risks

## Open loops
1. Confirm final canonical payment endpoints per silo.
2. Define exact approval artifact template path.
3. Stand up recurring ledger review cadence.
4. Assign fulfillment owner per active offer.
5. Build local FAQ for repeated troubleshooting.

## Top risks
- Payment outage during intent peak
- Public action without approval proof
- Silo data leakage
- Missing proof artifacts for key runs
- Scaling before paid-signal stability

## Mitigations
- Daily payment surface check
- Approval deny-by-default enforcement
- Separate silo directories and credentials
- Mandatory proof and verifier for meaningful scripts
- Weekly winner/loser review with kill decisions
