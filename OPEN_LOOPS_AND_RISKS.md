# OPEN LOOPS AND RISKS

## Open loops
1. Confirm payment surfaces for each active offer.
2. Confirm approval-record template is in active use.
3. Confirm silo folder and credential separation in production paths.
4. Confirm daily verifier habit is active.
5. Confirm weekly trifecta review cadence.

## Risks
- drift back to memory-driven operations,
- scaling before paid signal,
- silent public actions with no approval file,
- silo leakage through shared tooling,
- stale ledgers due to skipped runs.

## Mitigations
- run installer monthly to refresh canonical pack,
- run verifier before business day start,
- require written approvals for public outputs,
- block cross-silo automation by path policy,
- audit ledgers weekly.
