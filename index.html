BrownEye Cortex Cashflow Factory Blueprint

Prime directive: increase legal, observable cashflow quickly while reducing human cognitive load. Outputs are factories, not projects.

Task 1

Top 5 monetization leaks (value already generated)

  • High-signal prompts and wordbanks that never reach a payment surface at the moment of intent.
  • Repeatable research and arbitrage findings not packaged into a low-friction SKU.
  • Internal stencils that create assets but do not emit a proof artifact with a buy link.
  • Automation logs that show demand signals but do not feed pricing or bundles.
  • Cloned templates that ship without a prebuilt checkout or tip jar exposure.
Task 2-3

Repeatable SKU factories (3)

  • SKU Factory A: Prompt-to-Asset Pack
    • Produces: 10 themed prompt packs plus 3 ready-made output samples per pack.
    • Proof artifacts: D:\Proof\PromptPacks\YYYYMMDD\pack.json, pack.zip, manifest.txt, hash.txt.
    • Safe rerun: idempotent folder per date; overwrite only within that date folder.
    • Payment exposure: Stripe link embedded in pack.json and in README.txt.
  • SKU Factory B: Stencil-to-SKU Cloner
    • Produces: 25 SKU stencils cloned into unique variations with pricing metadata.
    • Proof artifacts: D:\Proof\Stencils\YYYYMMDD\stencils.zip, manifest.csv, hashes.txt.
    • Safe rerun: deterministic naming via hash of stencil inputs.
    • Payment exposure: Wise and Stripe URLs appended to manifest.csv.
  • SKU Factory C: Signal-to-Offer Converter
    • Produces: 15 offer pages and 1 bundle page with tiered pricing.
    • Proof artifacts: D:\Proof\Offers\YYYYMMDD\offer-*.html, bundle.html, sitemap.xml.
    • Safe rerun: versioned folders with immutable outputs; no manual cleanup.
    • Payment exposure: embedded checkout buttons on every page.
Task 4

Reliability and observability gaps

  • Gap: silent automation failure. Guardrail: heartbeat log written to D:\Proof\Heartbeat\status.json with last run time, exit code, and output counts.
  • Gap: missing proof verification. Guardrail: hash all proof artifacts and append to D:\Proof\Manifests\hashes.txt; alert if hash file is missing.
Task 5

Money-while-idle loop (72 hours)

  • Loop: every 2 hours, generate 5 new prompt packs, update a rolling bundle, and rotate a featured SKU.
  • Proof: each run writes a dated folder, manifest, and hash list in D:\Proof\IdleLoop\YYYYMMDD\.
  • Payment: bundle page keeps always-on Stripe and tip jar links; no manual edits required.
  • Degradation: if any generation step fails, reuse last known good assets and log the failure.
Output validity checklist: proof artifact exists, rerunnable without cleanup, payment surface included, and future work reduced.
