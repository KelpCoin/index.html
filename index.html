Monetization Sprint Pack — 7 Days to Sellable Inventory

Day 1: Offer Selection Matrix
Score 5 ideas on urgency, audience size, price elasticity, and time-to-deliver. Select the highest-scoring offer. Output: 1-page offer brief.

Day 2: Asset Blueprint
Define the core deliverable (template, checklist, script pack, or guide). Output: structured outline with sections, formats, and examples.

Day 3: Production Sprint
Build the full artifact. Use the Quality Gate: zero TODOs, clear buyer outcome, formatted for immediate sale.

Day 4: Listing + Sales Copy
Create headline, bullets, benefits, and FAQs. Output: Gumroad-ready listing plus a 150-word launch post.

Day 5: Distribution Flywheel
Schedule 3 channels: Discord announcement, Patreon member update, and 1 social thread. Output: copy/paste messages.

Day 6: Pricing & Bundling
Set a base price + bundle upsell. Add VIP perk for Patreon/Discord (exclusive office hours or bonus templates).

Day 7: Launch + Feedback Loop
Launch to core audience, capture FAQ responses, and record 3 improvements for v1.1.
