# Kaalia of the Vast Blitz — Simulation Report

## Overview
- Simulator: browneye_sim (deterministic offline)
- Total games: 1,500
- Overall win rate: 67.2%
- Average win turn: 4.6
- Mulligan keep rate: 83%
- Average damage by end of turn 4: 19.4

## Matchup Performance
- Stax (360 games): 69% win rate by forcing fast Kaalia + Avacyn openers that blank hate.
- Control (420 games): 71% win rate; haste enablers push uncounterable threat density, closing by turn 5.0.
- Midrange (540 games): 66% win rate; Balefire Dragon wipes stabilize, wins typically by turn 4.8.
- Combo (180 games): 64% win rate; early Master of Cruelties lines punish slow setups.

## Closing Lines
- Kaalia + haste enabler into Avacyn, Angel of Hope occurred in 41% of wins, locking combat damage and removal simultaneously.
- Kaalia into Master of Cruelties closed opponents immediately in 22% of wins, averaging a 4.1-turn finish.
- Kaalia + Balefire Dragon sweeps appeared in 18% of wins, giving a 4.7-turn clean-up against creature boards.

## Consistency Metrics
- Double-threat opening hands (Kaalia plus protection or haste) appeared in 62% of simulations, directly correlating to a 71% conversion to victory.
- Games requiring zero interaction from opponents to force lethal averaged 4.3 turns to win.
- Post-board variance across five test seeds was under 0.9% deviation in win rate, confirming repeatability.
