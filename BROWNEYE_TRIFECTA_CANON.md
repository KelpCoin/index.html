# BrownEye Trifecta Canon

The BrownEye Trifecta is the canonical operating model.

## Leg 1: Payment Surface
There must always be a live, testable path to pay.

## Leg 2: Signal Capture
Paid and intent signals must be recorded in append-only ledgers.

## Leg 3: Fulfillment Reliability
Delivery after payment must be clear, repeatable, and auditable.

## Trifecta pass conditions
- Payment path is reachable and tested.
- Signal events are logged with timestamps.
- Fulfillment path has owner and response standard.

## Trifecta fail conditions
- No active payment destination.
- Missing or broken ledger capture.
- Unowned or undocumented fulfillment.

## Operational rule
If any leg fails, freeze scale actions until repaired and verified.
