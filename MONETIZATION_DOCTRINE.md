# MONETIZATION DOCTRINE

## Core doctrine
Money is lost when there is nowhere to pay at intent peak. Payment surfaces are infrastructure, not garnish.

## Binding rules
1. Payment-first scaling.
2. No scale before paid signal.
3. Every campaign or offer has a direct pay path.
4. Remove friction between intent and checkout.
5. Do not spend attention on unpaid vanity metrics.

## Practical sequence
1. Publish offer and payment surface.
2. Validate paid intent with real transactions.
3. Only then increase traffic, content, or ad spend.
4. Clone proven funnels and offers.
5. Kill unproven work rapidly.

## Paid signal definition
A paid signal is a completed transaction captured in ledger and reconciled with source of traffic.

## Minimum evidence packet
- offer identifier
- payment URL or checkout endpoint
- transaction count and value
- source channel
- timestamped proof file
- ledger entry

## Stop rules
Stop a campaign if:
- no paid signal after planned test window,
- payment surface breaks,
- unit economics go negative without recovery path.
