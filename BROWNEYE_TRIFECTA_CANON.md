# BROWNEYE TRIFECTA CANON

## Canonical trifecta
1. Offer and payment surface live.
2. Distribution engine active.
3. Measurement with proof and ledger active.

All three are required. Missing one invalidates scale.

## Trifecta checks
- Offer: clear value proposition and direct checkout.
- Distribution: controlled channel with repeatable traffic.
- Measurement: proof artifact plus append-only JSONL entry.

## Operating rule
Scale only when trifecta integrity is green for at least one full test cycle with paid signal.

## Drift prevention
- weekly trifecta review
- archive old playbooks
- keep one canonical playbook per active winner
